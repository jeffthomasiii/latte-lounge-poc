# Latte & Lounge Brand Guides

These four practical guides are the shared reference for Latte & Lounge brand, website, and content decisions. They are intentionally concise enough to use during day-to-day work and detailed enough to keep future additions consistent.

## The Guides

1. [Creative Foundation](CREATIVE_FOUNDATION.md)  
   Defines who Latte & Lounge is, who it serves, how it should feel, and the principles that guide creative decisions.

2. [Brand Style Guide](BRAND_STYLE_GUIDE.md)  
   Defines the logo, color, typography, photography, graphic language, and visual guardrails.

3. [Website Design System](WEBSITE_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md)  
   Defines the reusable website patterns, component behavior, layout standards, responsive rules, and implementation guidance.

4. [Content Standards](CONTENT_STANDARDS.md)  
   Defines Samatra's voice, messaging hierarchy, content pillars, writing structures, Scripture use, calls to action, and editorial review standards.

## Source-of-Truth Order

When guidance conflicts, use this order:

1. These current brand guides
2. `docs/_data/brand.yml` for reusable public-facing brand language
3. Current approved website templates and styles for implemented behavior
4. Earlier project notes and historical brand documents

The website may use practical implementation alternatives where licensed brand assets are not available. Those alternatives must preserve the same mood, hierarchy, and intent.

## Core Brand Statements

- **Primary tagline:** A sacred pause before the world gets loud.
- **Secondary tagline:** Where women meet God before the world gets loud.
- **Brand promise:** Anchored instead of anxious.
- **Core message:** Set your day with God before your day begins setting you.
- **Public audience line:** Professional women who love God and carry a lot.

## Maintenance

Update a guide when a decision becomes established and repeatable, not for every experiment. Temporary design explorations may remain in branches or issues until they are approved.
