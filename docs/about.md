    <p class="ll-section-label">Why this space exists</p>
    <h2>I needed God’s wisdom before I walked into the work, the conversations, and the unknowns waiting for me.</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="editorial-intro-copy">
    <p>I’m Samatra, and Latte &amp; Lounge was born during a demanding transition in my professional life.</p>
    <p>I had stepped into regional leadership, where the responsibility was heavier, the decisions carried more weight, and I could not always turn to someone else to help me figure out what came next.</p>
    <p>Most mornings, that meant sitting at my kitchen island with a cup of coffee, talking honestly with God, praying over what I was carrying, and letting everything hang out before the day began.</p>
  </div>
</section>

  <div class="editorial-split-section__copy">
    <p class="ll-section-label">Why Latte &amp; Lounge</p>
    <h2>A real morning ritual. A relaxed conversation with God.</h2>
    <p>I love coffee, espresso, and a beautifully crafted latte. But the name means more than a morning drink.</p>
    <p>For me, the latte is part of a real morning rhythm. The lounge is the freedom to relax in God’s presence and talk with Him as my Lord, my Savior, my friend, and the One who loves me most.</p>
    <p>This is not about a prescribed routine. You do not need the perfect setting, the perfect prayer, or an uninterrupted hour. The invitation is simple: come to God honestly and bring Him what you are carrying.</p>
  </div>
</section>
