    <p class="ll-section-label">Why this space exists</p>
    <h2>I needed God’s wisdom for the decisions I had to make, the conversations I had to have, and the situations I could not predict.</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="editorial-intro-copy">
    <p>I’m Samatra, and Latte &amp; Lounge was born during a demanding transition in my professional life.</p>
    <p>I had stepped into regional leadership. My responsibilities had grown, the decisions carried more weight, and I could no longer turn to someone else every time I needed help figuring out what came next.</p>
    <p>Most mornings, that meant sitting at my kitchen island with a cup of coffee, talking openly with God, praying through what was on my mind, and bringing Him the parts I had not yet figured out.</p>
  </div>
</section>

  <div class="editorial-split-section__copy">
    <p class="ll-section-label">Why Latte &amp; Lounge</p>
    <h2>A real morning rhythm. An honest conversation with God.</h2>
    <p>I love coffee, espresso, and a beautifully crafted latte. But the name means more than a morning drink.</p>
    <p>For me, the latte is part of a real morning rhythm. The lounge is the freedom to relax in God’s presence and talk with Him as my Lord, my Savior, my friend, and the One who loves me most.</p>
    <p>This is not about a prescribed routine. You do not need the perfect setting, the perfect prayer, or an uninterrupted hour. The invitation is simple: come to God as you are and tell Him what is true.</p>
  </div>
</section>

    <p>Latte &amp; Lounge is beginning with written reflections, prayer resources, podcast conversations, and stories from professional women. The long-term vision is to create a place where women can find mentorship, leadership development, coaching, and a community committed to helping them grow.</p>
    <p>I care deeply about women entering new levels of leadership without the preparation, wisdom, or support they deserve—especially women of color who may be stepping into leadership without the preparation, mentorship, or support others have received.</p>
