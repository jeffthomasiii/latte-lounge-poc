I’m Samatra, and Latte & Lounge was born during a demanding transition in my professional life.

I had stepped into regional leadership, where the responsibility was heavier, the decisions carried more weight, and I could not always turn to someone else to help me figure out what came next. I needed God’s wisdom before I walked into the work, the conversations, and the unknowns waiting for me.

Most mornings, that meant sitting at my kitchen island with a cup of coffee, talking honestly with God, praying over what I was carrying, and letting everything hang out before the day began.

I started noticing something: **when I set my day with God, the trajectory of my day changed.**

And I realized that if I did not set my day, my day would set me.

## Why Latte & Lounge

I love coffee, espresso, and a beautifully crafted latte. But the name means more than a morning drink.

For me, the latte is part of a real morning ritual. The lounge is the freedom to relax in God’s presence and talk with Him as my Lord, my Savior, my friend, and the One who loves me most.

This is not about a prescribed routine. You do not have to have the perfect setting, the perfect prayer, or an uninterrupted hour. You may be sitting quietly with a journal, getting dressed for work, or moving through a full morning. The invitation is the same: come to God honestly and bring Him what you are carrying.

## Who this is for

Latte & Lounge is for professional women who love God and carry a lot.

You may be single or married, a mother or not a mother, an employee, entrepreneur, manager, emerging leader, or experienced decision-maker. Your responsibilities may look different, but you know what it feels like to wake up thinking about the work, people, conversations, and needs already waiting for you.

This is a place to step back from the list long enough to remember: **you get to spend time with God before everyone else starts needing something from you.**

## What you can expect

Latte & Lounge brings together Scripture, prayer, raw and real conversations, and practical wisdom for faith, work, leadership, relationships, and everyday decisions.

Faith is not one separate part of life here. It is the lens through which we learn to lead, respond, choose, serve, and live.

You will not find pretense or the suggestion that anyone has it all together. Think of this as sitting in the kitchen with an auntie who loves God, tells the truth, listens without judgment, and shares what she has learned because she genuinely wants another woman to grow.

## Where this is going

Latte & Lounge is beginning with written reflections, prayer resources, podcast conversations, and stories from professional women. The larger vision is to become a place where women can also find mentorship, professional development, growth, and coaching.

I care deeply about women entering new levels of leadership without the preparation, wisdom, or support they deserve—especially women of color who may be navigating unfamiliar rooms without an established guide.

Meaningful success is not simply building an audience. It is helping women recognize their potential and one day say, “Someone at Latte & Lounge believed in me, shared what she had learned, and helped me move toward who God was calling me to be.”

**A sacred pause before the world gets loud. A place to become anchored before the day begins.**
