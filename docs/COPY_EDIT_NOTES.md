# Latte & Lounge Copy-Editing Pass

This branch applies the approved site-wide editorial recommendations after the reusable About and Blog system was merged.

Key standards applied:

- Replace unnatural constructions such as “walked into the work” and “entered the work.”
- Use concrete language instead of generic wellness or coaching phrases.
- Reduce repetition of “carry,” “honest,” and “day ahead.”
- Standardize Reflections, Podcast, Prayer Resources, and newsletter calls to action.
- Distinguish current content offerings from future mentorship, coaching, and leadership-development plans.
- Preserve Samatra’s direct, warm, kitchen-table voice.
- Keep the official brand spelling as “Latte & Lounge.”
