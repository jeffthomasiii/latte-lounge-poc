Hi, I’m Samatra

I know what it feels like to lead, transition, and still need God’s direction.

Latte & Lounge began during a demanding leadership transition. As the weight of responsibility grew, I learned that when I met honestly with God before beginning my day, the trajectory of the day changed.

I did not need a perfect routine. I needed to bring Him the truth, ask for wisdom, and let Him anchor me before I entered the work, decisions, and people ahead.
