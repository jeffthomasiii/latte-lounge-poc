Hi, I’m Samatra

I know what it feels like to step into greater responsibility and realize how much you need God’s direction.

Latte & Lounge began during a demanding leadership transition. As my responsibilities grew, I learned that when I met openly with God before beginning my day, the trajectory of the day changed.

I did not need a perfect routine. I needed to tell Him the truth, ask for wisdom, and let Him steady me before I faced the responsibilities, decisions, and people depending on me.
