Monday can carry a lot before it even begins.

The work, decisions, conversations, deadlines, and people who need something from you may already be running through your mind.

In this Set Your Day episode, we bring the real Monday morning to God. We pray over the workweek, ask for wisdom and steadiness, and place the pressure before Him before it starts directing how we think and respond.

## Episode Prayer

Lord, before this week begins moving quickly, I bring You everything already on my mind.

Give me wisdom for the decisions I need to make, grace for the people I will encounter, courage for the conversations I cannot avoid, and peace for what I cannot control.

Help me lead, work, serve, and respond through a biblical worldview. Keep me steady when pressure rises, and remind me that I do not have to carry this week alone.

I trust that You hear me. Help me set this day with You and walk into it anchored instead of anxious.

Amen.
