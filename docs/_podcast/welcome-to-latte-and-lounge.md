Welcome to Latte & Lounge, where women meet God before the world gets loud.

I’m Samatra, and this space is for professional women who love God and carry a lot.

Latte & Lounge began when I realized that the mornings I spent talking honestly with God—often over a cup of coffee at my kitchen island—changed the trajectory of my day. If I did not set my day, my day would set me.

This podcast is an invitation to do that together through Scripture, honest prayer, raw conversations, practical wisdom for faith and leadership, and stories from women willing to share what they have learned.

You do not need a perfect routine or polished words. Bring God what you are carrying, and let’s prepare for the day ahead.
